    About

    StickerFace hides faces in a photo with an emoji sticker, a blur patch or a mosaic patch. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step, no bundler, no framework, and no network requests after the page loads. Nothing leaves your device.

    Credits:

    • Emoji artwork: Twemoji by X Corp. and contributors, licensed CC-BY 4.0 — see assets/emoji/ATTRIBUTION.md. StickerFace is MIT, the artwork is not.
    • Face-detection ideas and design lessons from ORB-HD/deface (MIT) and kmcheung12/faceblind (MIT).

    Export re-encodes the photo through a canvas, so EXIF and GPS metadata are removed. Video is not supported.
